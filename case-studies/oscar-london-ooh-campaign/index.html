The challenge

A visibility problem before it is a conversion problem.

When OSCAR launched in the UK, it needed to become more than a new app people could download. It needed to become a brand Londoners recognised.

Launching a new consumer app into an established market is a visibility problem first. OSCAR needed to introduce itself to London and establish familiarity quickly, targeting homeowners and busy professionals aged 25–54.

The campaign needed to make OSCAR feel present in the city, not hidden behind another digital ad competing for attention on a phone screen.

The objective

A strong London presence, quickly.

Why OOH

Seen in everyday life, not only on a phone.

“We wanted people to see OSCAR in everyday life rather than just another advert on their phone.”

That is where out-of-home earns its place. Instead of waiting for consumers to open an app, scroll a feed or search for a service, OSCAR was placed directly into the environments where Londoners were already spending time.

The Tube

Frequency and repeated exposure during everyday journeys on the Northern, Elizabeth, Jubilee and Central lines.

The street

High-profile 6-sheets around major destinations, plus Overground billboards that extended the footprint beyond the Underground.

The media strategy

Reach and repetition, not a single format.

The creative idea

Humour, built like an online review.

OSCAR supplied the creative concept. Three executions used the familiar language and visual cues of online reviews to create funny, slightly uncomfortable stories around everyday home-service problems.

That approach suited out-of-home. The audience did not need a long explanation. A quick read delivered the setup, the punchline and the OSCAR proposition. Instead of looking like a conventional service advert, the executions felt closer to the comments and reviews people already recognise.

In a busy Tube environment, that distinction matters. The creative had to work quickly, and the humour gave people a reason to stop, look twice and remember the brand.

Loud! OOH’s role. OSCAR owned the creative idea. Loud! OOH turned that idea into a scaled London campaign, selecting a media mix that could deliver broad exposure. The principle was simple: make the brand visible where Londoners already are, combining high-frequency transport media with prominent roadside and pedestrian-facing locations.

The result

10,200,000 impacts in two weeks.

For a brand entering the UK market, that gave OSCAR a substantial first burst of visibility across some of London’s busiest transport environments.
