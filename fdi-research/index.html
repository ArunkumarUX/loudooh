Methodology and evidence

The six-company starter watchlist sits above a full Fortune Global 500 (2024) list of 500 ranked names. Rank, HQ, sector and revenue come from the Fortune ranking page; footprint, developments, opportunity and priority on the watchlist are sourced from official company or UAE government pages, with analyst inferences for Dubai FDI engagement.

  • Confirmed: a named company, entity or government source supports the footprint claim.
  • Partial evidence: the source supports an ecosystem, reseller or regional signal but not a confirmed corporate office.
  • Verify: legal entity names and Dubai leadership are often not public in secondary sources and must be validated against Dubai free-zone / mainland registries before outreach.
