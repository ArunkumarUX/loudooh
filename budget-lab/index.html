How the estimate works

Where do the prices come from?

From the published rates on our Insights pricing guides. Where a guide shows a range, the Budget Lab uses the middle figure. Prices are media costs per unit, excluding VAT.

How are impressions estimated?

Lower weekly impressions per unit, multiplied by the quantity you choose, multiplied by the number of campaign weeks. We use the lower estimate on purpose, and we only estimate impressions for formats and quantities you have actually selected, never from budget alone.

Why are there minimum quantities?

Some formats are only sold in packs. Tube car panels, for example, are sold as a line pack of 558 panels, and taxi supersides need a minimum fleet. The Budget Lab applies these minimums automatically.

What do printing and production costs cover?

Printing covers the posters or vinyl for static formats. Production covers posting and installation. Digital formats have no printing or posting cost. Creative design is not included.

Is this a quote?

No. It is a rough estimate to help you plan. Availability, exact sites and final rates are confirmed in a detailed media quote after you get in touch.

Is this a live booking system?

No. Nothing is booked. Send us your estimate and we will come back with live availability and a detailed media quote.
