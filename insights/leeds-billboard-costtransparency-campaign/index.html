The problem

Opaque pricing is the real barrier - not the billboards.

Ask five agencies what a 48-sheet costs and you get five answers, none written down. Loud! OOH publishes every rate. The challenge: nobody believes transparency until they see it.

What the market does

Hide prices behind forms and discovery calls. Markup layers make SMEs assume outdoor is out of reach.

What we did instead

Stopped explaining. Booked two adjacent panels on York Street at our own published rate - and put the comparison on the wall.

The stunt

The medium was the message.

York Street, Leeds - bus stop dwell in front of both boards. Read left to right. No competitor named. The industry markup culture was the villain.
